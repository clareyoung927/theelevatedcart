Mini wreaths on the cabinets, red striped towels and one bowl of ornaments. The kitchen is done.

How do you decorate a kitchen for Christmas?

Hang mini wreaths on the upper cabinet doors, swap in red and white striped towels, set a pedestal bowl of red ornaments on the counter, add a tree-shaped serving board and put pine branches in a crock. Keep most of the counter clear.

The kitchen is where everyone ends up at a Christmas party, and it is the easiest room to decorate because the cabinets do the work. Hang small wreaths, swap the towels and add one bowl on the counter.

The 6 pieces, in order

  1. Mini cabinet wreaths, set of 6. Hang them on the upper doors with ribbon over the top of the door. No nails.
  2. Red and white striped KitchenAid towels. Hang one on the oven handle and one on the dishwasher.
  3. A Christmas tree wood serving board left out on the counter with cheese and rosemary.
  4. A white footed pedestal bowl as the counter centerpiece.
  5. Red ball ornaments to fill the bowl.
  6. Norfolk pine branches in a crock by the stove.

The styling rules

  • Wreaths on every other door if you have a lot of cabinets.
  • Keep the counters 80 percent clear so you can still cook.
  • Stick to red, white and green so it reads classic, not cluttered.

Frequently asked questions

How do you hang wreaths on kitchen cabinets?

Tie them to a ribbon looped over the top of the cabinet door, no nails needed.

What is the easiest Christmas kitchen decor?

Mini cabinet wreaths and holiday towels change the room in minutes.

What colors work for Christmas kitchen decor?

Classic red, white and green reads clean in a white kitchen.
