How do you set up a hot cocoa station?

Use one tray to corral everything, keep mugs within reach, put toppings like marshmallows and peppermint in clear jars, and add a little greenery.

A cocoa station turns a corner of the counter into a holiday moment.

The formula

  1. One tray to corral everything.
  2. Mugs within reach.
  3. Toppings in clear jars: marshmallows, peppermint, cinnamon sticks.
  4. A little greenery to soften it.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need for a hot cocoa bar?

A tray, mugs, cocoa, and toppings in clear jars.

Where should a cocoa station go?

A corner of the kitchen counter or a cleared section of a bar cart.

What toppings go on a hot cocoa bar?

Marshmallows, crushed peppermint, chocolate chips and cinnamon sticks.
