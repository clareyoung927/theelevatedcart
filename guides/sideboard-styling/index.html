How to Style a Sideboard

Anchor, height and something living.

This post contains affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Learn more

How do you style a sideboard or buffet?

Center a round mirror or large art as the anchor, add height on one side with a tall vase or candleholder, soften it with greenery, and balance the other side with a smaller grouping.

A sideboard is a stage. Give it a backdrop and a little rhythm.

The formula

  1. Anchor: a round mirror or a large piece of art, centered.
  2. Height: a tall vase or candleholder off to one side.
  3. Living: branches or greenery to soften the edges.
  4. Balance: a smaller grouping on the opposite side.

Keep it asymmetrical

Perfect symmetry looks staged. Let one side be taller than the other.

Frequently asked questions

What goes above a sideboard?

A round mirror or a large piece of art centered over it anchors the arrangement.

Should sideboard decor be symmetrical?

Slightly asymmetrical looks more natural. Let one side be taller than the other.

How wide should a mirror be over a buffet?

About two-thirds the width of the sideboard.
