The measurement that fixes most living rooms.

How high should you hang art above a sofa?

Hang it so the bottom of the frame sits 8 to 10 inches above the top of the sofa back, and choose art or a grouping about two-thirds the width of the sofa.

Most art is hung too high. The fix is a single number.

The rule

Leave 8 to 10 inches between the top of the sofa back and the bottom of the frame.

How wide

Aim for art or a grouping that spans about two-thirds of the sofa's width. One large piece, a pair, or a grid of four all work.

For a gallery grid

Keep 2 to 3 inches between frames and treat the whole group as one piece when you measure.

Check it first

Tape paper templates to the wall and live with them for a day before you put in a nail.

Frequently asked questions

How wide should art be over a couch?

About two-thirds of the sofa's width. One large piece, a pair or a grid of four can all hit that width.

How far apart should frames be in a gallery grid?

Leave 2 to 3 inches between frames and measure the whole grid as one piece.

Is it better to hang art too high or too low?

Too high is the most common mistake. Keeping art close to the sofa connects the two into one arrangement.
