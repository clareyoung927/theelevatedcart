About

Hi, I'm Clare. Welcome to The Elevated Cart.

This is where the ideas from my Pinterest boards get the full story: the recreated rooms, the measurements, the layering order and the small details that make a room feel finished.

I love the look of Pottery Barn, Arhaus and those warm, lived-in Nancy Meyers kitchens. I don't love paying for it. So I recreate those rooms with pieces that genuinely match the inspiration in shape, material and finish, and I only share ones I've checked.

Every guide works without buying a thing. If you'd like the exact pieces, every room and guide links straight to them.

Thanks for being here.
