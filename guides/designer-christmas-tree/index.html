Why does my Christmas tree look flat?

Tuck lights deep inside along the branches, add wide ribbon in loose cascades, and hang ornaments in three sizes: large ones deep inside, medium in the middle and small on the tips.

The difference between a pretty tree and a designer tree is depth.

Lights first

Wrap lights deep inside along the branches, not just around the outside. Aim for about 100 lights per foot of tree.

Ribbon next

Tuck wide ribbon in loose cascades from top to bottom.

Ornaments in three sizes

Hang large ornaments deep inside, medium in the middle and small on the tips. It reads as fullness.

Frequently asked questions

How many lights do you need on a Christmas tree?

A common guide is about 100 lights per foot of tree.

How do you put ribbon on a Christmas tree?

Tuck wide ribbon in loose cascades from top to bottom rather than wrapping it around.

Where do big ornaments go on a tree?

Hang the largest ornaments deeper inside the branches to add depth.
