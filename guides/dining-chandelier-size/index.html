How to Size a Dining Chandelier

The two numbers that make a chandelier look right.

What size chandelier do you need over a dining table?

Choose a fixture about half to two-thirds the width of the table, and hang the bottom 30 to 36 inches above the tabletop.

A chandelier that's too small is the most common dining room miss.

Width

Aim for a fixture about half to two-thirds the width of the table. For long tables, consider two smaller fixtures.

Height

Hang the bottom of the fixture 30 to 36 inches above the tabletop.

Shortcut

No table yet? Add the room's length and width in feet. The total, in inches, is a good fixture diameter.

Frequently asked questions

How high should a chandelier hang over a dining table?

The bottom of the fixture should sit 30 to 36 inches above the tabletop.

How do you size a chandelier without a table?

Add the room's length and width in feet. The total, in inches, is a good fixture diameter.

Should a long dining table have two lights?

Yes, for long tables two smaller fixtures often look better than one oversized one.
