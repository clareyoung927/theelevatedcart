Why your rug feels small, and the size that fixes it.

What size rug do you need for a living room?

Most living rooms need an 8x10 or larger so at least the front legs of every seat sit on the rug. In a dining room, the rug should extend about 24 inches past the table on every side.

A too-small rug makes a whole room feel off. When in doubt, size up.

Living room

At least the front legs of every seat should sit on the rug. For most rooms that means 8x10 or larger.

Dining room

Add about 24 inches past the table on every side so chairs stay on the rug when pulled out.

Bedroom

The rug should extend 18 to 24 inches past each side of the bed.

Frequently asked questions

Should furniture legs go on the rug?

At minimum the front legs of the sofa and chairs should rest on the rug so the seating area feels connected.

How big should a rug be under a dining table?

Add about 24 inches on every side of the table so chairs stay on the rug when pulled out.

How big should a rug be under a queen bed?

It should extend 18 to 24 inches past each side of the bed, which usually means an 8x10.
