How do you hide a Christmas tree stand?

Use a woven tree collar or a large basket wide enough to cover the stand with a few inches to spare, then layer a throw or wrapped gifts around the base.

The tree stand is the one thing that makes a beautiful tree look unfinished.

The fix

Then layer

Add a chunky throw or a few wrapped gifts in kraft paper and ribbon around the base.

Frequently asked questions

Is a tree collar better than a tree skirt?

A collar gives a cleaner, more structured look, while a skirt feels softer. Both hide the stand.

What size tree collar do you need?

Choose one a few inches wider than your stand.

What can you put around the base of a Christmas tree?

A chunky throw, a basket or gifts wrapped in kraft paper and ribbon.
