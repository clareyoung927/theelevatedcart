How do you set a simple Thanksgiving table?

Start with a linen runner or tablecloth, lay fall branches low down the center, add candles under glass at a few heights, and finish with woven placemats and fringe napkins. Keep the centerpiece below eye level.

A memorable table is mostly about light and texture, not a centerpiece you can't see over.

The recipe

  • A linen runner or tablecloth in a warm neutral.
  • Fall branches or greenery laid low down the center.
  • Candles under glass at a few heights.
  • Woven placemats, fringe napkins and simple napkin rings.

The rule of low

Keep everything in the center below eye level so guests can talk across the table.

Frequently asked questions

How tall should a Thanksgiving centerpiece be?

Keep it below eye level, roughly under 12 inches, so guests can talk across the table.

What do you need for a Thanksgiving tablescape?

A runner or tablecloth, a low centerpiece, candles, placemats and cloth napkins.

How do you make a Thanksgiving table look elevated on a budget?

Use real texture: linen, woven placemats, greenery and candlelight do most of the work.
