The Coffee Table Formula

Something tall, something low, something living.

How do you style a coffee table?

Use three groupings: something tall like a vase, something low like a stack of books with a small object on top, and something living like branches or a plant. Keep about a third of the surface open.

Styling a coffee table is easier with a formula.

The three groupings

  1. Tall: a vase or candlestick.
  2. Low: a stack of two or three books with a small object on top.
  3. Living: branches, a plant or a bowl of fruit.

Leave room

Keep about a third of the surface open. A coffee table still needs to hold coffee.

Frequently asked questions

How many items should be on a coffee table?

Three groupings is the sweet spot. More than that starts to look cluttered.

What do you put on a round coffee table?

Center one tall piece, then place a low stack and something living around it in a loose triangle.

How do you make a coffee table look expensive?

Mix materials like ceramic, wood and greenery, and leave open space around each grouping.
