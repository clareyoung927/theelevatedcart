Frequently asked questions

What is a dupe for the Pottery Barn Washington Spruce tree?

The Goplus 8 ft pre-lit aspen tree has a similar full, natural shape with warm white lights and two-tone tips.

How tall a tree do I need?

Leave about 6 to 12 inches between the top of the tree and the ceiling, plus room for a topper.

How do I make an artificial tree look real?

Fluff every tip, tuck lights deep along the branches and add a few real cedar sprigs or a pine-scented candle nearby.
