How do you decorate a table for Christmas?

Layer round woven placemats, classic Christmas tree dinner plates and tartan cloth napkins, then run a cedar garland down the center with brass taper holders and white candles. Keep the centerpiece under 14 inches.

A Christmas table looks collected when it mixes one pattern, one texture and one classic dish. Tartan is the pattern, woven placemats are the texture, and tree plates are the classic.

The 6 pieces, in order

  1. Round woven placemats. Natural texture under every plate keeps the red and green from feeling loud.
  2. Christmas tree dinner plates with green trim. The one piece everyone recognizes.
  3. Tartan plaid cotton napkins folded flat on each plate. A 12 pack covers a full table plus spares.
  4. A real touch cedar garland down the center as the runner.
  5. Brass taper holders along the garland.
  6. White taper candles. Unscented at the table, always.

The styling rules

  • Keep the centerpiece under 14 inches so guests can see each other.
  • One pattern only. If the napkins are tartan, everything else is solid.
  • Set it the night before so the day of is easy.

Frequently asked questions

What is a simple Christmas table centerpiece?

A cedar garland down the middle of the table with taper candles along it.

How tall should a Christmas centerpiece be?

Under about 14 inches so guests can see each other.

Can you mix plaid napkins with patterned plates?

Yes, if everything else on the table stays solid.
