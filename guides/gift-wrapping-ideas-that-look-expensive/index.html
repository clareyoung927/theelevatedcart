How do you make gift wrapping look expensive?

Use one plain paper like brown kraft on every gift, tie it with velvet ribbon in two alternating colors, add a plain kraft tag and tuck a dried orange slice into the bow.

Expensive looking gifts are not about fancy paper. They are about one plain paper, one rich ribbon and one natural detail, repeated on every gift so the pile under the tree looks planned.

The wrapping kit

  1. Brown kraft paper, 36 inch roll. Wide enough for boxes, and one roll lasts all season.
  2. Burgundy velvet ribbon. Velvet is what reads expensive.
  3. Dark green velvet ribbon for every other gift.
  4. Plain kraft tags on twine. Write names in black pen.
  5. Dried orange slices tucked into the bow.

Gifts under $50 that fit the look

  1. WoodWick vanilla sea salt candle. The crackling wick is the gift.
  2. Cotton cable knit throw. Works for anyone with a couch.
  3. Scalloped marble cheese board. Looks far more than it costs.
  4. White footed pedestal bowl. A host or new homeowner favorite.

The rules

  • Same paper on every gift, alternate the ribbon colors.
  • Wrap the ribbon both ways and tie the bow on top, off center.
  • Cut ribbon ends at an angle so they do not fray.

Frequently asked questions

What ribbon looks most expensive?

Velvet ribbon in burgundy, deep green or brown.

Is kraft paper nice for Christmas gifts?

Yes, plain kraft paper with velvet ribbon looks elevated and costs very little.

What are good Christmas gifts under $50?

A WoodWick candle, a cable knit throw, a marble cheese board or a pedestal bowl.
