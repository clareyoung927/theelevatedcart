How do you decorate a coffee table for Christmas?

Start with one large round tray, add a low cedar garland, three brass taper candles at different heights, a candle ring for a touch of red, a short stack of books and a few bottle brush trees. Keep everything under 12 inches and leave a third of the table clear.

The Christmas coffee tables that look expensive all follow the same rule: one large tray holds everything, and nothing is taller than a candle. People sit around this table, so keep it low enough to talk over and clear enough to set down a mug.

The 7 pieces, in order

  1. A big round wood tray. At 17.7 inches it fills the middle of a standard 48 inch coffee table. Everything else sits on it or right beside it.
  2. A 6 ft real touch cedar garland. Lay it loosely along the back edge or coil it around the tray. Faux cedar looks real at arm's length, which matters on a table people sit next to.
  3. Three brass taper holders. Different heights read as styled, not lined up. Put them on the tray.
  4. White taper candles. Unscented and dripless, so they will not compete with dinner.
  5. A pine candle ring. Drop one around a pillar candle for the only bit of red on the table.
  6. Two neutral decorative books. Stack them off center and set something small on top.
  7. Bottle brush trees. A cluster of three in different heights next to the books is the playful moment.

The styling rules

  • Keep everything under 12 inches tall so faces stay visible across the table.
  • Group in odd numbers: three candles, three trees.
  • Leave a third of the table empty for drinks and feet.
  • One accent color only. Here it is the red berries in the candle ring.

Frequently asked questions

What do you put on a coffee table for Christmas?

A tray, a garland, candles, a stack of books and one small playful piece like bottle brush trees.

How do you decorate a coffee table for Christmas without clutter?

Corral everything on one tray, group items in threes and leave a third of the surface empty.

How tall should coffee table decor be?

Under about 12 inches so people can see each other across the table.
