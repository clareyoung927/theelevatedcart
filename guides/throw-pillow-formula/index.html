The Throw Pillow Formula

Three textures, two sizes, one lumbar.

This post contains affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Learn more

How do you arrange throw pillows on a sofa?

Place two 22-inch squares at each end, two 20-inch squares in a second texture in front of them, and one lumbar in the middle. Stay in one color family and vary the textures.

Pillows are the fastest way to make a sofa look finished, and the easiest place to overdo it.

The formula for a sofa

  1. Two 22-inch squares in a solid or subtle pattern at each end.
  2. Two 20-inch squares in a second texture in front of them.
  3. One lumbar in the middle to tie it together.

Mix textures, not colors

Stay in one color family and vary the surface: linen, boucle, faux fur, a woven stripe. It reads layered without looking busy.

Go down-filled if you can

Use inserts 2 inches larger than the cover. That's what makes the classic "chop" hold its shape.

Frequently asked questions

How many throw pillows go on a sofa?

Four to five: two pairs of squares plus one lumbar works on most standard sofas.

What size insert should you use for a pillow cover?

Use an insert about 2 inches larger than the cover for a full, plump look.

How do you mix pillow patterns?

Keep one color family and vary scale and texture, such as a solid linen, a woven stripe and a boucle.
