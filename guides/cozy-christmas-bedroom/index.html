How do you decorate a bedroom for Christmas?

Swap in plaid or flannel pillow covers, add a chunky knit throw at the foot of the bed, hang a small wreath above the headboard and use warm lamp light.

The bedroom is the easiest room to make feel like the holidays.

The swap

  • Plaid or flannel pillow covers on the shams.
  • A chunky knit throw at the foot of the bed.
  • A small wreath above the headboard.
  • Warm lamp light instead of overheads.

Frequently asked questions

What is the easiest way to make a bedroom feel festive?

Holiday pillow covers and a chunky throw change the look in minutes.

Can you hang a wreath over a bed?

Yes, a small wreath on a ribbon above the headboard is a simple, classic touch.

What lighting makes a bedroom cozy?

Warm table lamps instead of overhead lights.
