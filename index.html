About

Hi, welcome to The Elevated Cart.

This is where the ideas from my Pinterest boards get the full explanation: the measurements, the layering order, and the small details that make a room feel finished.

I love the look of Pottery Barn, Arhaus and those warm, lived-in Nancy Meyers kitchens. I don't love paying for it. So I look for pieces that genuinely match the inspiration in shape, material and finish, and I only share ones I've checked.

Every guide works without buying a thing. If you'd like the exact pieces, there's a short shopping list at the end, and my full storefront is always one click away.

Thanks for being here.
