How do you set up an entryway drop zone?

Give it four parts: a bench for shoes, hooks at two heights, one basket per person and a tray for keys. Give every item one home so it stays clear.

A great entry does three jobs: somewhere to sit, somewhere to drop and somewhere to hang.

The formula

  1. A bench for shoes.
  2. Hooks at two heights, low ones for kids.
  3. One basket per person.
  4. A tray for keys.

Keep it clear

Give each item one home. If it doesn't fit in the basket or the tray, it moves on. A mirror above is the finishing touch.

Frequently asked questions

What do you need in a small entryway?

A bench or slim console, hooks, a basket for shoes and a tray for keys.

How high should entryway hooks be?

Hang adult hooks around 60 to 66 inches and a lower row for kids.

How do you keep an entryway from getting cluttered?

One basket per person and one tray for keys, and anything that doesn't fit moves on.
