How I shop Black Friday for my home

  1. Big pieces first. A sale on a sideboard or rug can save far more dollars than a sale on a candle. Start with the furniture you already planned to buy.
  2. Measure before you buy. Check the size against your room so you are not returning a rug in January. My rug size guide and art above the sofa guide help.
  3. Check the deal badge and the price. Amazon marks deal prices on the listing. Prices change during the week, so compare before you check out.
  4. Order Christmas pieces early. Trees, ornaments and ribbon are the first to sell out.
