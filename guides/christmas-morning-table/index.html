How do you set a Christmas morning breakfast table?

Set it the night before with a linen runner, woven placemats, plaid or fringe napkins with simple rings, greenery in a low vase and a hurricane candle in the center.

Set the table on Christmas Eve and the morning takes care of itself.

The setup

  • A linen runner and woven placemats.
  • Plaid or fringe napkins with simple rings.
  • Greenery in a jar or a low vase.
