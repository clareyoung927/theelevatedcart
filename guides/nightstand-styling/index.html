Light, a little lift and one personal thing.

How do you style a nightstand?

Start with a lamp tall enough that the shade sits around shoulder height when you sit up in bed, add a small stack of books for lift, and finish with one personal item. Keep chargers in a drawer.

A nightstand needs to be useful first. Then it can be pretty.

The essentials

  • A lamp tall enough that the shade sits around shoulder height when you're sitting up in bed.
  • A small stack of books for lift.
  • One personal item: a small vase, a framed photo or a candle.

Hide the rest

Choose a nightstand with a drawer so chargers and lotion stay out of sight.

Frequently asked questions

How tall should a bedside lamp be?

When you sit up in bed, the bottom of the shade should sit around shoulder or chin height.

What should go on a nightstand?

A lamp, a short stack of books and one personal piece such as a small vase or framed photo.

Do nightstands need to match?

No. Pieces in a similar height and tone look collected rather than matched.
