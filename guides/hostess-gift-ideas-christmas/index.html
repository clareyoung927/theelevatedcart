What do you bring a hostess who has everything?

Bring something she can use after the party or keep for years: Christmas cloth napkins, linen cocktail napkins, holiday tea towels, a marble wine cooler with a bottle inside, a Spode Christmas Tree serving piece or a Lenox ornament.

You asked what to bring and she said she has everything covered. You still do not want to show up empty handed. The best hostess gifts do one of two things: help her clean up after the party, or become part of her Christmas every year after.

Things she will use that night

  1. Embroidered Christmas cloth napkins, set of 4. Tie with a ribbon and hand them over.
  2. Linen hemstitch cocktail napkins, 12 pieces. Perfect for the drinks table.
  3. A white marble wine cooler. Bring it with a bottle inside and it is a two-in-one gift.
  4. GEOMETRY Christmas wreath tea towels, set of 4. Quick dry, so they actually get used.

Things she will keep for years

  1. Spode Christmas Tree trivet. The classic pattern she may already collect.
  2. Spode Christmas Tree 3-piece serving set. Serving fork, spoon and slotted spoon.
  3. Lenox 2026 annual ornament. Dated, so it marks the year of the party.
  4. Lenox cardinal ornament. A meaningful one for anyone who has lost someone.
  5. Lenox Santa fire truck ornament. For the hostess with kids or a firefighter in the family.
  6. Lenox snow globe tree ornament. The safest pick if you do not know her style.

How to give it

  • Hand it to her with a quiet "this is for you, not for tonight" so she does not feel she has to use it right away.
  • Put a tag on it with your name. Hosts get a lot of gifts in one night.
  • Under $50 is the sweet spot for a hostess gift.

Hostess gifts under $30 that look expensive

On a budget? These look like they came from a kitchen boutique. Most are under $30, so you can pair two.

  1. A 14 inch olive wood cheese board. Every piece has its own grain, so it feels one of a kind.
  2. A marble and wood appetizer board with a knife. Ready to use the night of the party.
  3. A marble and gold cheese knife set. Tuck it under the ribbon on the cheese board for a two-part gift.
  4. An amber glass scented candle. The hobnail jar looks far pricier than it is.
  5. A glass olive oil bottle. Fill it with good olive oil and tie on a tag.
  6. A textured ceramic vase. Bring it with a few stems of eucalyptus or pine.
  7. Acacia salad servers. Warm wood that goes with every kind of dinnerware.

Frequently asked questions

How much should you spend on a hostess gift?

Under $50 is the sweet spot.

Is an ornament a good hostess gift?

Yes, a dated annual ornament marks the year of the party and she will keep it.

Should the hostess open the gift at the party?

No, tell her it is for her to enjoy later, not for tonight.

What is a good hostess gift under $30 that looks expensive?

An olive wood cheese board, a marble and gold cheese knife set, an amber glass candle or a textured ceramic vase with a few stems all look boutique for under $30.
