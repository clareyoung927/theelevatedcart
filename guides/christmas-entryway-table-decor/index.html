How do you decorate an entryway table for Christmas?

Center a mirror over the console and hang a wreath on it with velvet ribbon, flank it with a matching pair of lamps, and add one tall vase of pine branches off center. Keep the middle clear for keys and mail.

The entry console is the first impression of your whole house at Christmas. The formula is simple: symmetry on the outside, one tall moment off center, and a wreath that hangs on the mirror instead of the door.

The 7 pieces, in order

  1. A round gold mirror centered over the console. It bounces light into a dark entry.
  2. A 20 inch Norfolk cedar wreath hung on the mirror. Twenty inches covers about half a 30 inch mirror, which is the right ratio.
  3. Burgundy velvet ribbon to hang the wreath. Loop it over the top of the mirror and leave long tails.
  4. A pair of marble-base buffet lamps, one at each end. Matching lamps are what make a console look finished.
  5. A large rustic ceramic vase set off center, between the mirror and one lamp.
  6. 36 inch Norfolk pine branches in the vase. Tall stems give height without a tree.
  7. Vintage gold bells on jute tied around the neck of the vase.

The four-layer Christmas console formula

No room for seven pieces? Use four layers: light (one lamp, or a pair), greenery (a low cedar or pine garland running along the top), something gold (a brass bowl of clementines, a small brass reindeer or candlesticks) and one thing to hold the gifts (a woven basket underneath with wrapped presents). A round mirror above pulls it together.

The styling rules

  • Lamps first, then the mirror, then the one tall arrangement.
  • Keep the middle of the console clear for keys and mail.
  • Turn the lamps on at dusk. Lamp light is what makes an entry feel like Christmas.

Frequently asked questions

How big should a wreath on a mirror be?

About half the width of the mirror. A 20 inch wreath suits a 30 inch round mirror.

What goes on a Christmas console table?

A pair of lamps, a mirror with a wreath, one tall arrangement and a small accent like bells.

How do you hang a wreath on a mirror without nails?

Loop a long ribbon over the top of the mirror frame and tie the wreath to it.

Can you decorate a Christmas entryway for under $200?

Yes. A fluted console table, a round gold mirror, a small ceramic lamp, a cedar garland and a woven basket come to about $200 together on Amazon as of November 2026.
