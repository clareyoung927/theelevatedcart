How do you make a living room cozy for Christmas?

Turn on tree and garland lights and table lamps, light candles under glass, turn the overhead lights off, and add throws and holiday pillow covers to every seat.

The coziest holiday rooms are lit low.

Layer the light

  • Tree lights and garland lights on.
  • Table lamps on.
  • Candles under glass on the coffee table.
  • Overheads off.

Then add softness

A throw on every seat and a few holiday pillow covers make the room feel ready for company.

Frequently asked questions

What makes a holiday room feel cozy?

Low, layered light from the tree, lamps and candles instead of overheads.

How do you decorate a living room for Christmas on a budget?

Swap pillow covers, add throws and lean on lights and candles.

Should you use warm or cool Christmas lights?

Warm white lights feel cozier and blend with lamplight.
